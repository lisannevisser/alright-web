Privacy Policy

Effective: August 2, 2026

Short version

alright doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no server run by us. Everything the app stores lives on your device.

What alright stores, and where

  • Your settings (chosen notification time, language, visual style, appearance) are saved locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage (UserDefaults).
  • The daily affirmations are bundled with the app itself — nothing is downloaded or fetched from a server.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device with Apple's notification system (UNUserNotificationCenter). We never see when or whether a notification was delivered or opened.

If your device backs up to iCloud or your computer, that backup is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms — it's not something alright initiates or controls.

Third parties

None. alright contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests. We don't share data with anyone, because we don't collect any to share.

Retention and deletion

Your settings stay on your device until you change them, or until you delete the app, which removes everything alright stored. There's no account to close and no server-side copy to request deletion of, because none exists.

Notification permission

You can turn notifications on or off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → alright.

Children

alright doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version at this same address.
